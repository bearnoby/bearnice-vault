# Preference: lube

Source: MEMORY.md, Personal care & shopping.

- Wants **liquid, not gel**. Astroglide was the benchmark.
- Glycerin dries tacky (Ritex; JO H2O a partial improvement).
- pjur AQUA 100 ml = **PZN 00791148**. Always quote the PZN; he was sold the wrong product once.
- Silicone: thinner is better (pjur LIGHT), never with silicone toys.
- He's spent a lot, so don't push upgrades unasked. The site's cheat sheet has a `lube/` subpage.
- See [[concepts/ordering-online-over-speaking-german]].
