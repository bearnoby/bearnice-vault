# Letter taste

Bear's standing preferences for everything the letters writer writes for him: cover letters, form answers and messages to recruiters. The writer gets every bullet under "Taste" with every request; what he asks for in a thread wins over them. Edit them here freely: one rule per bullet, plain words. When he corrects something in a thread that holds for every role, the post asks "Remember this for every role?" and his ✅ adds it at the end of the list. Started on 24 Nov 2026 from his corrections in the N8N, Lovable, ClickStack and Wise threads. System: [[entities/systems/cover-letters|Cover letters]].

## Taste

- Don't open with "I applied because" or any other throat-clearing; say what I'm applying for and get to the point.
- No phrasing nobody would say out loud (like "making scheduled AI-driven work run reliably"). Write it the way a person talks, not as a sales pitch.
- "Why us" and "what caught your attention" answers are warm and about the company and what it makes: why I'd want to work there. Not a pitch of my skills.
- A "message to the hiring team" is logistics first (where I am, work permit, when I'm available), then a brief bridge of skills and motivation, in a human tone.
- My Python Twitch desktop toolkit is not AI or LLM work; never present it as such.
- Don't over-claim: no stretching what I did to fit the ad.
- When my own AI tool use is relevant, frame it as real enthusiasm (I build things with Claude Code, so I believe in the mission), not as professional expertise.
