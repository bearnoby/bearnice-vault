# Decision: where to relocate (place ladder)

Source: MEMORY.md, Job hunt & relocation.

- Netherlands first (not Rotterdam), then Norway, Sweden, Denmark, Germany, Belgium, then Ireland and the UK.
- France is out. The south is too hot. Eastern Europe only if remote pay is high.
- English-only; another language is a plus, never a requirement.
